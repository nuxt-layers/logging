# Storage register

Process-local diagnostic history: bounded trusted tenant/source, codes, opaque correlation, numeric/boolean fields and timestamps; delivery states and local restriction flag. Purpose technical diagnostics. Host-configured retention at most 30 days, lazy expiry and process termination. No durable storage, cookies or browser storage. Protected local export discloses only scoped sanitised records; local erasure/restriction do not alter provider data. Independent approval references returned, not retained by Logging. Host local/external sinks independently document location, retention, access, holds, deletion and backups. Rate buckets and global health counters are process-local. Cache is not legal preservation custody; durable hold, provider rights and lifecycle evidence remain unavailable. Do not deploy for workloads requiring them.
